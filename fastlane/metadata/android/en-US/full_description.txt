Voice Notes records a note with one tap and turns it into text on your phone. Nothing is uploaded: no account, no cloud, no analytics, no ads.

It is built for use while driving: start a note from a Quick Settings tile, a home-screen widget, a Bluetooth headset or steering-wheel play/pause button, or from Android Auto, speak, and stop. Other audio pauses while you record and resumes afterwards.

<b>Features</b>
• Offline speech recognition with Whisper (whisper.cpp); the language is detected automatically among the languages you prefer, such as English, Dutch and French
• Newest or oldest first, with a header for each day
• Colour themes: follow the system (as before), Light, Dark, Paper, High contrast, Matrix and Dracula, each tested for readable text
• Your own labels on notes, and one search bar: words search the text, label:name searches only the labels
• Backup and restore to a file you choose (optionally encrypted with a passphrase); nothing is sent by the app itself
• Several versions of every transcript: re-transcribe with another model or language, edit by hand, go back
• Recordings with no speech (silence, road noise) are skipped instead of producing invented text
• Waits while the battery is low or battery saver is on, then continues
• Share text or audio, add notes to a Markdown file you choose (an Obsidian vault, a Nextcloud folder), or export everything
• Optional recording through a Bluetooth headset or a car's hands-free system
• You choose and download the speech model (0.5 to 0.9 GB); you can also import one from a file

<b>Good to know</b>
• Needs Android 14 or newer and a 64-bit ARM phone. The best model needs about 1 GB of free memory; older phones work but transcribe slower.
• Android Auto lists apps from outside Google Play only if you switch on its developer option "Unknown sources". There is no note-taking category in Android Auto, so the app appears as a media app with a Record button.
• In the car, start a note with the Record button on the Android Auto screen. The car's voice button goes to Android Auto's assistant, not to this app; without an assistant on your phone, Android Auto says "Voice commands are not available at the moment".
• The only network use is downloading a speech model (and a small speech-check model) from huggingface.co when you ask for it. You can switch the network permission off completely and import models from a file instead.
